What this does and does not do

  • Payments follow the French system (constant installment), the standard in Spain. On a rate review or a "cut the payment" repayment, the installment is recast over the remaining term.
  • Euribor is the one-year official rate published by Banco de España (series ), embedded with this page and refreshed by hand. The newest month can be provisional until it appears in the BOE. Nothing here fetches live data.
  • Taxes are the general 2026 rates by region. Reduced rates (young buyers, large families, VPO, disability) are not modeled, and the taxable base can be the cadastral reference value if higher than the price. Notary, registry, gestoría and appraisal figures are typical estimates; adjust them above.
  • Under Law 5/2019 the bank pays the notary, registry, gestoría and AJD of the loan deed itself, so only the purchase side is counted here.
  • This is a simulator, not financial advice. A bank's binding numbers come in its FIPRE/FiCARE pre-contractual documents.
